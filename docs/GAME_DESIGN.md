# I'M DOG? — Game Design (brief)

**One line:** a funny, cozy third-person game about an ordinary day, lived as Moke, a small white Maltipoo.

## Pillars
1. **Being Moke feels good.** Running around the room is fun before there's any objective.
2. **Small dog, big world.** Camera height and true-scale furniture make you feel small.
3. **Dog logic is the humour.** *Sock? Interesting. Owner wants sock? Interesting. Sock gets treat? SOCK = TREAT.*
4. **Cozy, never stressful.** No combat, no death, no survival meters, no violence.

## Tone and look
Warm, soft, colourful, slightly mischievous, family friendly. A high-quality stylized animated-film look with
anime-inspired expressiveness: head tilts, happy squints, readable ears and tail. Not photoreal, not chibi,
not crude low-poly. Moke must read as *that specific dog* (see `MOKE_CHARACTER_REFERENCE.md`).

## Identity
Title: **I'M DOG?**, with exact capitalization, apostrophe and question mark. It's a playful nod to the "I'M DONUT?"
shop name, with an entirely original identity: rounded Fredoka lettering, the "O" drawn as Moke's fluffy round head,
a coral "?" that tilts like a curious dog, and a small katakana tag (アイム・ドッグ？) for the Japanese flavour.
Palette: warm cream, cocoa ink, coral, leaf green.

## Core verbs
Walk, trot, run, look, bark, sniff, pick up / carry / drop, push toys, lie down / get up.

## Future mini-games (architecture allows them, not built yet)
- **Sock Heist:** steal a sock, get noticed ("Moke!"), run, hide, trade the sock for a treat.
- **Perfect Nap:** find the best spot by comfort, warmth, noise and safety.
- **Treat Hunt:** use Sniff Mode to find hidden treats.
- **Doorbell:** race to the door, bark, chaos.
- **Where Is My Human?:** search the house by sound and smell.
- **Backyard Patrol:** birds, squirrels, leaves, neighbour dogs.

## Out of scope for now
Humans/NPCs, full house, backyard, other animals, weather, day/night, economy, quests, multiplayer,
accounts, backend, mobile controls, consoles.
